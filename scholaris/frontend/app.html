Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate my digital student ID card?

Once your registration form is submitted through the Student Section, your holographic 3D ID badge is minted automatically. You can also view, print, or download it anytime under the "My Digital ID Badge" tab by searching your Roll Number.

What is the minimum attendance requirement?

As per SRMIST university statutory academic regulations, all students must maintain at least 75% aggregate attendance in each registered course to be eligible for end-semester university examinations.

Who can access the College Management section?

The College Management section is exclusively restricted to verified SRMIST professors (including Dr. Anjali Sharma, Dean Prof. R.P. Mahapatra, and HODs) and administrative registrar officers with secure authentication credentials.
