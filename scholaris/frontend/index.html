Process Overview

Step-by-Step Registration Procedure

Follow these four simple steps to complete university enrolment and generate verified student identification.

1

Fill Registration Details

Enter student profile, previous academic marks (10th/12th), guardian contacts, and permanent residential address.

2

Quota & Seat Validation

System automatically checks branch seat capacity and validates roll number uniqueness server-side.

3

Digital ID Card & QR

A verified digital student identity card is instantly minted with scannable QR verification and watermark signature.

4

Roster Allotment

The student is immediately added to the active branch roster for daily attendance tracking and examination records.
