Admissions Roadmap

5-Stage Structured Enrollment Flow

Designed to ensure complete demographic, academic merit, and guardian verification for every registered student.

1

Personal Profile

Full legal name, date of birth, gender, blood group, social category, and Aadhar/national ID details.

2

Academic & Branch

10th & 12th board percentages, qualifying entrance ranks, target engineering branch, and batch allotment.

3

Parent Particulars

Father's & mother's full names, occupations, emergency contact numbers, and parent email addresses.

4

Contact & Address

Student phone number, institutional email address, permanent residential address, and postal PIN code.

5

Services & ID Badge

Hostel preference, campus bus route selection, declaration, and instant digital ID badge generation.

Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common admissions, documentation, ID badge, and campus life questions.

What documents are required during online admission registration? ▼
You will need your 10th Standard Certificate, 12th/Qualifying Board Examination Marks Sheet, Entrance Examination Scorecard (if applicable), Social Category Certificate, and Aadhar/National Govt ID.
How is the student digital ID badge generated? ▼
Upon submitting the online registration form through the Student Portal, your official Roll Number is assigned and a digital ID badge with QR code verification is minted instantly. You can view or print it anytime.
What is the university minimum attendance requirement? ▼
As per statutory SRMIST university academic guidelines, students must maintain at least 75% aggregate attendance in all registered theory and laboratory courses to remain eligible for end-semester examinations.
Who can access the College Management section? ▼
The College Management section is exclusively restricted to verified SRMIST professors (including Dr. Anjali Sharma, Dean Prof. R.P. Mahapatra, and HODs) and administrative registrar officers with secure single sign-on credentials.
