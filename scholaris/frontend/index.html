Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about admissions, document verification, hostel facilities, and fee payments.

What documents are required during online registration? ▼
You will need your 10th Standard Marks Card, 12th/Qualifying Examination Certificate, Entrance Examination (JEE Main/SRMJEEE/State Merit) Scorecard, Transfer/Migration Certificate, Social Category Certificate (if applicable), and Govt ID (Aadhar Card).
How is the Student Digital ID Badge generated? ▼
Upon submitting the 5-step registration form in RegEngine, the server assigns your verified Roll Number and instantly mints a 3D Holographic ID Badge with an encrypted QR verification code and academic watermark.
Are hostel accommodations and bus transportation available? ▼
Yes! SRMIST Delhi-NCR Ghaziabad provides separate AC and Non-AC hostel residences for boys and girls on campus, as well as dedicated college bus routes covering Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, and Ghaziabad metro stations.
What is the minimum attendance requirement? ▼
As per university academic regulations, students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered theory and laboratory course to be eligible for end-semester examinations. RegEngine automatically flags attendance defaulters.
